Our Testing Events in Luxembourg

Passion and ongoing training fuel our constant search for ways to unite the testing community in Luxembourg. Between our events, such as the
Luxembourg Software Testing Event
and our
Meetup Group
Group, our pleasure lies in sharing and discussing the latest trends and innovative tools in software testing. Join us at our events!

Luxembourg Software Testing Event

The #LSTE
a Q-Leap initiative!

Our annual Luxembourg Software Testing Eventhas been in existence for several years and has a clear objective: to bring together the software testing community for a half-day of fun. conferences, workshops and networking events ! Immerse yourself in the latest trends, share your knowledge and seize the opportunity to create synergies in the exciting world of software testing.

Meetup

Luxembourg Testing & Quality Assurance group Meetup

The Luxembourg Testing & Quality Assurance group is THE must-attend Meetup! We created it to share knowledge on software quality assurance and testing. From time to time, we get together for Meetups, to network and chat about the software quality management landscape in Luxembourg. Everyone is welcome, whether to take part in the event or as a speaker!
