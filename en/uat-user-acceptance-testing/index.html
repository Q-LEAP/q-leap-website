User Acceptance Testing

UAT tests

software testing services

Tests to ensure that the application meets its requirements.

UAT (User Acceptance Testing) is a crucial step in the software testing process, where end-users test the application to ensure that it meets their needs and is ready for production. This phase ensures that the software is user-friendly, operates in line with user expectations, and is ready for deployment.

Automated testing

Use of automated tools and scripts to perform repetitive tests and ensure exhaustive coverage of application functionalities. Automated testing can speed up the process and ensure consistent execution of scenarios.

Manual testing

Tests carried out by human testers to evaluate the application from the point of view of end-users. This can include validating functionality, user interface and real-life usage scenarios.

Test planning & design

Development of detailed test plans, defining test cases, scenarios and acceptance conditions. Test design provides a structured approach to the testing process.

Recruit users and train the team

Recruitment of end-users to form a dedicated UAT team representing the real users of the application.

Report & Analysis

Provision of detailed reports on test results, identifying problems detected, areas of risk, and providing recommendations for improving the user experience.
Added value

The benefits of UAT testing

Investing in accessibility testing (UAT testing) today has several major advantages, including:

01Validation by end-users: ensuring that the product meets their real needs and expectations.

02Early detection of problems: reducing subsequent correction costs.

03Improved product acceptance: ensure that the product is ready for use and accepted by end-users, thus improving adoption.

04.continuous optimization: enabling ongoing optimization of solutions over successive deployments.

05Risk reduction: by validating the product before deployment, UAT minimizes the risk of negative post-launch feedback.

Answers to software testing challenges

UAT tests are designed to proactively identify potential obstacles that could hinder an application's ability to ensure optimal use for all users. Among other things, they address these issues:

  • Resolve problems before commissioning, to avoid negative feedback and the consequences of launching a faulty product
  • Access easy-to-use testing tools at your fingertips, so you can concentrate on improving your software without worrying about test management.
  • Determine exactly what you want our UAT testers to evaluate: specific aspects of your product or the entire product, initiating test cycles as required.
  • Use of expert UAT testers, highly motivated to detect problems and usability aspects, without being hindered by cognitive biases or tempted to simply say what you want to hear.
