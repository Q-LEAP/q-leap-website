Q-LEAP Factory: Flexibility & Rates at least 30% cheaper than an in-house consultant.

Do you have a project requiring testers but don’t want to make a long-term commitment?

With our Factory, there’s no need to recruit full-time staff. We take charge of your project and mobilize a team of specialized testers, tailored to your needs. All areas of expertise are covered: functional, non-functional and automation testing.

We offer credits in the form of man-days for one tester, providing total flexibility to suit your needs.

How does our Factory work?

Customer request

The customer provides a detailed description of its needs or project, specifying expectations, specifications and objectives.

Estimation & Validation

A senior tester analyzes and prepares an estimate of the cost, time and resources required. The customer then validates it.

Buying credits

The customer buys the credits needed to finance the project. They are then valid for one year, at any time.

Start & Feedback / Report

The project starts, regular feedback is given and a final report is provided with results and recommendations.
