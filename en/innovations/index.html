innovation for better testing

For all testers.

At Q-LEAP, innovation is truly at the heart of our identity, and we will continue to develop quality solutions to support our customers in their quest for excellence. Discover Q-Guard and Q-Bot, our innovative solutions for software test automation. We develop tailor-made tools, adapted to the concrete needs of testers, to simplify and improve the entire testing process.

Automate all tests

Q-Guard offers an innovative software test automation solution via its intuitive platform. By simplifying the testing process, it automatically identifies real-life application use cases , facilitating the creation of relevant test scenarios without the need for complex programming. Q-Guard can now be deployed locally (on-premise) and will soon be available as a SaaS solution.

Request a demo

Automate dual authentication

Q-BOT is a unique solution on the market that enables automatic recovery of the OTP (One Time Password) on all your tokens, even those from Luxtrust. With a single click, Q-BOT automates double authentication for all test cases. Q-BOT was created by testers, for testers.

In September, the Q-BOT Mobile version arrived, enabling us to test the 2FA for Luxtrust Mobile.
